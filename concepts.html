TAPÉ concept · pre-order view the brand →

one dispenser. every formula.

buy the device once. pop in whichever formula tape your skin needs that day — hydration, glow, firming, clarifying, or calm. the bet: separate the hardware from the refill, and let the routine flex with the skin instead of the shelf.

OUT concept · live visit out →

the bag you don’t mind seeing at the curb.

a trash bag brand that treats the most thrown-away object in the house as something worth designing. heavier-gauge, genuinely leak-proof liners in a considered palette — matte, muted, not the shiny white eyesore under every sink. the bet: the last honest product in the kitchen has gone unbranded for decades, and there’s a real one in making the boring necessity feel intentional.

COMB concept · in progress visit comb →

the honey jar, not the creatine tub.

a honey-based electrolyte fuel for athletes — real carbohydrate energy and electrolytes from honey, built as a clean alternative to synthetic creatine and lab-flavored powders. one ingredient you can pronounce, doing the work of the supplement aisle: fast fuel, hydration, and recovery for people who’d rather train on food than on a formula.

Fertility Calculator concept · live try the calculator →

the math nobody hands you in your twenties.

a plain-english calculator for fertility — egg quality and quantity by age, monthly conception probability, ivf and freezing cost math, the time and money trade-offs. built so the woman doing the planning can actually see the numbers, instead of getting them filtered through a clinic’s sales funnel.

Coffee Shop Model concept · reference download xlsx →

the math behind the brew.

operating & startup financial model for a 1,200 sq ft bed-stuy cafe. built for the folks who want to see the unit economics before they sign the lease.
